3.3. Thermal analysis of composite aluminum particles
The thermal reactivity and energy release properties of mAl,
mAl@PDA, and mAl@PF particles were tested by TG-DSC in an oxygen
atmosphere at a heating rate of 10 (cid:1)C/min, and the results are
shown in Fig. 6. The thermal decomposition process of mAl@Coating
particles can be divided into three stages: the oxidative decom-
position of polymeric coatings (Stage 1, 320e580 (cid:1)C), melting of mAl
(Stage 2, 580e690 (cid:1)C) and oxidation of liquid Al (Stage 3,
690e1300 (cid:1)C). For mAl@PF and mAl@PDA samples, the PVDF and
PDA coatings are thermally decomposed to produce gas products
(CO2, N2, and H2O) in a lower temperature range of 320e530 (cid:1)C
(Stage 1), resulting in mass losses of 6.81% and 1.95%, respectively.
As the temperature rises, two significant mass increase steps are
observed in the temperature range of 580e680 (cid:1)C (Stage 2) and
680e1300 (cid:1)C (Stage 3), respectively. For the mass loss step (Stage
2), the temperature is lower than the melting point of Al (660 (cid:1)C),
the slight weight losses of mAl@PDA, and mAl@PF samples were
mainly due to the decomposition of polymer coating [48,50], while
with further increase in temperature, the weight gain rate of mAl,
mAl@PDA, and mAl@PF was increased by 55.42%, 42.37%, and 56.47%
